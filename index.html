03 — The Ritual

Three movements,
four minutes.

Brewing is not a technique to master. It is a small room you return to, once or twice a day, where nothing else is required of you.

Press & hold to steep.

Four seconds here, four minutes in the pot. Release early and nothing is lost — only the moment.

  1. 01

    Heat 80°C

    Spring water, warmed until it still remembers the air. Never boiling — heat is a volume knob, not a switch.

  2. 02

    Steep 4 min

    Four grams, no more. The leaves open slowly; the room takes on the smell of rain on stone.

  3. 03

    Breathe ∞

    Pour in a spiral, leaving the last drop behind. Sit until the cup is cool in your hands.
